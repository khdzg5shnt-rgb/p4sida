Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Sparse Sampling and Borel Refinements of Closed Covers”** for publication as a research article in *Topology and its Applications*.

The paper asks how small the combinatorial sequence entropy can be when a finite Borel partition must refine a prescribed closed cover and the sampling times are chosen before the partition. For a map conjugate to a positive power of a mixing one-sided shift of finite type, it proves that every increasing sequence with gaps tending to infinity gives the exact minimum log N(K), where N(K) is the minimum subcover size. An ordered difference partition of a minimum subcover attains the value simultaneously for all these sequences and also minimizes maximal pattern entropy. The proof combines Baire category with finite-type concatenation to obtain actual orbit patterns for Borel atoms.

The comparison with earlier work is specific. Huang, Maass, and Ye (2004, Theorem 3.5) already established a common positive measure-sequence-entropy threshold over all measurable refinements under their hypotheses. The present result concerns an exact counting value for every prescribed divergent-gap sequence and arbitrary finite closed covers in the stated symbolic class. It also yields a sparse-time Borel refinement counterpart to Romagnoli’s (2007) cover–partition example; it does not settle the consecutive-time Borel refinement problem discussed there.

A fixed-period safe-control application retains all legal Borel feedbacks when legal blocks share their successor. A six-control example gives an exact full-class value of log 2 although the minimum safe-cover size is three, showing why the cover formula does not extend directly to feedback-dependent successors. The paper makes no claim to resolve the general feedback minimax problem.

The focus on closed covers, Borel partitions, category, and symbolic dynamics places the work within the journal’s scope in topology and topological dynamics. The manuscript provides complete proofs, an overlapping safety-domain example, and an explicit boundary to its applicability.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
